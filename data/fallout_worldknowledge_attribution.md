# Fallout World Knowledge Attribution

The basic Fallout world knowledge dataset in `fallout_worldknowledge_basic.csv`
contains concise summaries derived from articles on [The Fallout
Wiki](https://fallout.wiki/). The wiki publishes page content under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) unless otherwise
noted.

`fallout_worldknowledge_sources.jsonl` records the source article URL, page ID,
revision ID, revision timestamp, and generation model for every shipped topic.
The summaries were generated with `z-ai/glm-5.2` and reviewed by automated
format and content checks on July 20, 2026.
